Who We Are

India's Most Trusted Self-Drive Car Rental Platform

DriveEasy was founded in Mumbai in 2016 after our co-founders experienced a disastrous rental that left them stranded on NH48 with no customer support and a car that hadn't been maintained in months.

We set out to fix three things: car quality, transparent pricing, and genuine 24/7 support. Ten years and 200+ cars later, we serve 18,000+ customers across 50 Indian cities.

Our mission is simple — give everyone the freedom to drive, without the headache of ownership.

FAQ

Common Questions

What documents do I need to rent a car? +
You need a valid Indian driving licence (at least 1 year old), an Aadhaar card or passport for ID verification, and a debit/credit card for the security deposit.
Is there a minimum age requirement? +
Yes. You must be at least 21 years old with a valid driving licence held for at least 1 year. For luxury and SUV categories, the minimum age is 23.
What happens if I return the car late? +
Late returns are charged at 1.5× the hourly rate. You can extend your booking via the app any time before your return slot, subject to availability.
Is insurance included in the price? +
Yes — all our cars come with basic third-party insurance. You can upgrade to zero-deductible comprehensive coverage with our add-on for ₹149/day.
Can I drive the car outside my state? +
Yes, inter-state driving is allowed. Inform us at the time of booking — a small state permit fee may apply for some routes. Certain hill regions may require advance approval.
How is the security deposit handled? +
A refundable security deposit of ₹2,000–₹10,000 (depending on car category) is blocked on your card at pickup and fully released within 3 business days after return.
